# FeeFact

**Free TikTok Shop profit calculator & settlement fee audit** - know what you actually keep from every sale.

Website: https://feefact.com

## What it does

- **Profit calculator** - enter price, costs, creator commission and ad spend; see your real net profit per sale for TikTok Shop US, UK and EU, with referral fees at the official 6% vs the widely reported 8%.
- - **Settlement checker** - upload your settlement CSV and flag lines charged at a referral rate that differs from the expected one.
  - - **Batch SKU profit check** - paste a list of products and compare margins side by side.
    - - **Fee guides** - current TikTok Shop fees, fee changes and how to check your settlement, verified against official sources (rates last verified October 30, 2026).
     
      - ## Why
     
      - TikTok Shop fees are scattered across announcements and seller-center pages. FeeFact puts the numbers in one place so sellers can price with the real margin, not a guess.
